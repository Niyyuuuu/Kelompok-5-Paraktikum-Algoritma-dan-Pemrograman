It was great to be part of Group 5 for the Algorithms and Programming Lab in 2026! Together, we have completed 8 modules covering the Python and C++ programming languages. Starting with “Data Types and Variables in Python” and ending with “Functions in C++.” 

For our final assignment, we created an infographic about the characteristics and determinants of property valuation using the Housing dataset. The infographic visually interprets four main analytical categories: the impact of furnishing status on pricing, the premium value of homes with dual features (main road access and AC), the overall statistical correlation between structural attributes, and the frequency distribution of housing prices.

Through data-driven charts, our group highlights that property size (area) and essential modern facilities are the primary catalysts for higher real estate prices. This visual guide simplifies complex statistical trends into actionable insights, showing that fully furnished homes in strategic locations consistently command the highest market value.
