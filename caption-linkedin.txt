It was great to be part of Group 5 for the Algorithms and Programming Lab in 2026! Together, we have completed 8 modules covering the Python and C++ programming languages. Starting with “Data Types and Variables in Python” and ending with “Functions in C++.” 

For our final assignment, we created an infographic about the key factors influencing housing prices, analyzed from a comprehensive dataset of 545 property records. Our visualization highlights how crucial amenities—such as direct main road access, air conditioning, and complete furnishing—significantly drive up a property's market value. By breaking down these elements, we demonstrate how interior readiness and premium locations create a substantial price premium in the real estate market.

Furthermore, the infographic delves into the statistical correlations and distributions of the housing data. It illustrates that while features like the number of stories and parking spaces matter, a home's total land area and the number of bathrooms have the strongest positive correlation with its price. We also visualized the overall price distribution, revealing a right-skewed trend where the majority of properties are concentrated in the affordable-to-mid price range, providing a clear, data-driven perspective on real estate dynamics.
